About Me

Solar Design Engineer with Real On-Site Experience

5+ Years Field Experience

Hi, I'm Mikin — a solar power plant design engineer with over 5 years of hands-on experience in solar installation, design, and service.

I hold a degree in Electrical Engineering (BE), and I've spent the last five-plus years in the field — not just behind a desk. I've worked directly on solar installations, seen what works on-site and what doesn't, and carried that practical understanding into every design I create. That's what sets my work apart: it's not theoretical. Every layout, drawing, and calculation is built with real-world execution in mind.

Today, I work as a freelance solar plant design consultant, helping EPC companies, installers, and direct clients turn solar projects from concept to construction-ready design.

What I Do

End-to-End Solar Design Services

Whether you're an EPC company looking to outsource design work without compromising on quality, or a client who wants to see what your solar plant will look like before a single panel goes up — I offer complete design support.

2D & 3D Solar Layout

Precise panel layout and plant design for rooftop and ground-mount systems, optimized for maximum output and site constraints.

Shadow Analysis

Even a small shadow on one panel can pull down the output of an entire string. Hour-by-hour yearly shadow studies show exactly where panels can and cannot go.

PVsyst Report

Accurate, bankable estimates of energy yield, losses, and performance ratio — the report banks, clients, and inspection authorities trust.

Electrical SLD & Wiring

Single line diagrams, AC-DC wiring diagrams, and cable sizing calculations that decide your plant's output, safety, and lifespan for the next 25 years.

Client Presentations

Client-ready presentations and 3D visualizations that help your client see the finished plant — and say yes to the proposal.

AutoCAD Engineering Drawings

Detailed construction-ready engineering drawings that meet professional drafting standards, so your site team knows exactly what to build.
